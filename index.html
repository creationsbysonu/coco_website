02 — The story

A whole lot of cat. A little bit of chaos.

Coco is an orange tabby with a talent for turning ordinary afternoons into content. He started as a kitten with strong opinions about cardboard boxes and somehow became the internet's favorite orange correspondent.

These days he splits his time between sunbeams, brand campaigns and inspecting whatever you just put down. His work has one rule: if it isn't cozy, cute or a little bit ridiculous, it doesn't make the feed.

03 — The journey

A day with Coco.

Keep scrolling — the day moves sideways. Five stops, from the first sunbeam to lights-out.

01 / 05 06:00

The 6 a.m. alarm

Coco sits on the human's chest and paws at their eyelids until breakfast happens. Negotiation is not on the menu.

02 / 05 09:30

Sunbeam patrol

Every window in the house is inspected on a rotating schedule. The warm rectangle on the kitchen floor passes quality control.

03 / 05 13:15

Box QA shift

Any box that enters the house must be sat in, chewed at the corners and formally reviewed. Ratings average 4.9 paws.

04 / 05 17:40

Window commentary

Birds, leaves, one very suspicious pigeon. Coco provides running commentary at volumes only the neighbours can hear.

05 / 05 22:30

Lights out

Curl into the perfect donut, purr once for the road, and dream about tomorrow's sunbeam. Repeat at 6 a.m.
